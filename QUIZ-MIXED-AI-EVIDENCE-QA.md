# Mixed quizzes and printable AI review

Date: 2026-10-30. Base: `c31a9bda1ee173143cde8647d0b944cd1fce5e84`.

## Behavior

- `data-quiz-mode="mixed"` accepts drag-drop, number and choice questions in one set. Standard and drag-only sets keep their existing validation. The bilingual reusable example is `public/content/templates/quiz-mixed-template.html`.
- Answer scores and mastery still use the existing deterministic quiz grader. Missing working, an AI revision verdict, an unavailable service or an unexpected AI error never removes correct-answer credit. The missing-working dialog allows continuing with ordinary grading.
- Choice/number questions support typed reasoning, notebook ink and AI method review. Drag-drop questions use their slot answers, hide the reasoning panel and make no AI review requests.
- Results → Download evidence PDF includes answered questions, diagrams/context/options, submitted answers, separate ordinary answer results, typed working, ink, review feedback and per-question status stamps. Answered questions without working are included and marked as not reviewed. Drag questions have an ordinary answer-check label.
- Review status distinguishes passed, needs revision, not yet checked, unavailable and edited/recheck. A positive stamp requires an explicit positive service verdict without a real reported issue. Praise alone and ambiguous verdicts do not count as verification. Exact no-error placeholders such as `ไม่มีข้อผิดพลาด` do not falsely turn a positive verdict into a revision.
- Verification is tied to the current authored question, answer and work. Editing those invalidates a prior positive stamp; switching TH/EN alone does not. Old positive stamps without the current review policy/context metadata require a new review, while saved work is retained.
- Long question/working pages continue onto another A4 page with the question heading and stamp repeated. Text-line and image boundaries are preserved where possible. Scientific numeric answers include their authored unit.
- Blank worksheets, answer keys and summary printing continue to use the existing shared Print Core. Their blank/key documents do not represent a student's AI-reviewed submission; use the evidence report for stamps.

## Limits

- No Worker source, credentials, backend configuration or score policy was changed. Existing evidence-export completion behavior is retained.
- The review reference is generated by the client and is **not a server-signed certificate**. A stamp records the service review state for that saved work; it is not a guarantee against client-side tampering.
- Physical iPad/Safari, native printer output and device Save-to-Files handoff were not tested in this environment. PDF contents were checked from the actual generated PDF pages, rather than only from HTML previews.
- Existing quiz sets are not automatically rewritten into mixed sets. Authors can opt in using the new template and `data-quiz-mode="mixed"`.
