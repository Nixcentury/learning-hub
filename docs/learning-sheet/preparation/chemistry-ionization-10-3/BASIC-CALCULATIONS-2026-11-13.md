## Content decisions

All solutions refer to water at 25 °C; K𝓌 = 1.0×10⁻¹⁴. Concentrations use mol/L. Orbital-style direct substitution is adapted to chemical-equation ratios and equilibrium concentrations. The weak solutes are explicitly hypothetical one-proton acids/bases, preventing invented constants being assigned to real chemicals.

The two ICE tables are fully supplied, with equilibrium denominators 0.0990 and 0.048 mol/L. The main lesson's C₀/K > 1000 approximation criterion is retained. The approximation cases have ratios 2500 and 10000, with ionized fractions 2% and 1%; answers retain ≈. Conjugate constants refer only to the correct pair at the same temperature. Water's added ions are neglected for the solute-ionization questions, as stated. The sheet follows the school convention for displaying equilibrium constants without units.
