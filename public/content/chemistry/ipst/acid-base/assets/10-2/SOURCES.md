## Content boundaries and conventions

- Each pair differs by one H⁺ and one charge unit; the other atoms remain unchanged. The sign of a species alone does not determine its role.
- Reaction direction is stated. Source one-way arrows for HNO₃ and NH₂⁻ with water are retained; the other source equilibria use reversible arrows.
- The source's gaseous ammonia/methylamine input is retained where shown; the aqueous ammonia exercise uses its own source state. Some explanatory diagrams omit state symbols, without changing the pairing.
- The table's two dashes mean not supplied for that reaction set, not an assertion that H₃PO₄/H₂S can never be protonated in any condition.
- Amphiprotic is distinguished from the broader amphoteric term while retaining the book's terminology. Writing both possible reactions is not a claim of equal equilibrium extents.
- No Ka, Kb, Kw, pH calculation, relative-strength ranking, buffer or titration content is introduced in this section.
- No A-Level question is claimed as read or included. The current priority is complete IPST coverage and targeted additional practice.
