# Assets and provenance · Polymers L01–L02

All five SVGs are **new original diagrams** created specifically for this Learning Hub draft. They are not crops or redrawn copies of publisher illustrations. They use simplified educational models.

- `products.svg`: generic product icons to open the materials discussion, aligned with the IPST classroom observation activity (slides 3–5).
- `tests.svg`: original symbolic overview of the IPST material-property investigation (slides 6, 8–13), not actual test data. Heating and breaking are teacher-supervised only.
- `wire.svg`: original simplified cable cross-section used to discuss metal conductors and polymer insulation; not a construction specification.
- `monomers.svg`: original simplified molecular-chain model, based on concepts presented in Focus Smart Plus M3 Topic 3.1 page 5 and Aksorn M3 Vol. 1 Unit 3.
- `bonding.svg`: new conceptual model distinguishing mere proximity from chemical linking.

Source references for **ideas, not image reuse**:
1. User-provided `M3_3.1.pdf`, Focus Smart Plus, PDF pages 4–5 (opening and polymerization).
2. User-provided `m3b2_u5_02_1material_001_ppt.pptx`, IPST, slides 3–13 (products/properties/tests), 14 onward (polymer structure).
3. Aksorn supplementary teaching slides, `https://pubhtml5.com/enaz/fjzu/basic/`, Unit 3, Introduction and Polymers.

Important scientific QA: The original Focus wording "monomers of the same kind" is more restrictive than all polymers. Content uses **one or more monomer types**. The source slide's treatment of fat as a natural polymer is not repeated here (relevant to L03 later).
