# 19.1 Sheet 1 assets

- thermal-coffee-maker.jpg: Original paired thermogram/visible photograph by Torsten Henning, 20 September 2006. Public domain, as released by its author. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:CoffeeBrewerThermography_ty_20060920i4col0.jpg . Downloaded without modification. The image itself is not an exact temperature calibration: its author assumed emissivity 1 for every surface. Used for qualitative observation only.
- blackbody-spectra.svg: Newly plotted Planck spectral radiance curves at 3000, 4000, 5000 and 6000 K, on one common relative scale. Wavelength axis in nm, approximate visible band 400–700 nm. The relationships follow IPST Physics Book 6 Fig. 19.2; this asset is not a scan of that figure. The Planck-law expression is used only to generate a correct illustration, not taught as an additional formula.
- cavity-radiator.svg: Original explanatory diagram of a cavity radiator. English labels are shared by both language editions. Incident-radiation path and emitted-radiation path are distinct schematic rays.

The two numerical classroom activities are explicitly simulated data, following the quantum model and rounded h = 6.6e-34 J s. They illustrate the hypotheses; they are not Planck’s experimental measurements or independent evidence for quantization. Worked calculations use h = 6.626e-34 J s, c = 3.00e8 m/s and 1 eV = 1.60e-19 J.
