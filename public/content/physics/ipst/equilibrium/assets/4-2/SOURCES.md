# Physics 4.2 illustration sources

The teacher supplied the student book `715651630-ฟิสิกส-สสวท-เล-ม-2.pdf` and teacher guide `phy.c2560.2t.pdf` for this worksheet. Source PDFs remain unchanged and are not copied into the repository.

- `book-apparatus.png`: apparatus illustration only, cropped from the supplied student book, printed p. 6 / PDF p. 19. Shows the pencil pushing a book at different edge locations.
- `lamina-apparatus.png`: apparatus photograph only, cropped from the supplied student book, printed p. 9 / PDF p. 22. Shows the suspended irregular lamina and plumb line.
- All SVG files: original explanatory diagrams prepared for this worksheet, informed by the supplied book section 4.2 (printed pp. 5–10) and teacher guide (printed pp. 10–15). They are schematic, not experimental measurements.

`book-case-a.svg` and `book-case-b.svg` use the same book dimensions, initial orientation and force-arrow length. The force passes through the geometric centre in A and above it in B. B's later view rotates clockwise, consistently with that push. Grey outlines give the initial position in each later view. `book-lines.svg` shows only trials producing no initial rotation, with lines meeting at P.

All four lamina SVGs use the same uniform polygon, the same three material holes and a common exact area centroid. Each suspended orientation is calculated so that the selected hole is vertically above that centroid. The coloured lines are fixed marks on the sheet; previous marks rotate with it. The final diagram returns to the original orientation and marks the common intersection P. A third line checks the two-line location.

The ruler, block, ring and cylinder are explicitly uniform. The block's lines are body diagonals. The ring centre is in the empty region.

Additional source consulted for the suspension method (no image copied): Harvard Natural Sciences Lecture Demonstrations, “Irregular Lamina”, https://sciencedemonstrations.fas.harvard.edu/presentations/irregular-lamina .
