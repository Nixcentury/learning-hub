If the line density is \(N\) slits per unit length, the centre-to-centre separation is its reciprocal. For example, 500 lines/mm gives \(d=(1\ \mathrm{mm})/500=2.00\ \mathrm{\mu m}\).

For normal illumination the slit waves start in phase. In the far field the path difference between neighbours is \(d\sin\theta_m\). An integer multiple of \(\lambda\) puts all the slit contributions in phase.
