Classroom Note · 10.2 Double slit

1 · Observe the experiment and identify changes

Watch the demonstration → observe → explain briefly → try independently.

What do the two waves do?

Light diffracts through each opening. The two emerging waves interfere on the screen, producing many bright and dark fringes, rather than one fringe per opening.

Coherent sources: equal frequency and a constant phase difference. Ordinary independent sources do not maintain a stable pattern. Illuminating both slits from one source helps maintain their phase relationship.

Quick observation

Keep colour and screen distance fixed, then change slit separation. When are fringes closer? Now fix both distances and change colour. When are fringes wider?

Larger slit separation → closer fringes; longer wavelength → wider spacing. Change one variable at a time to identify its effect.
