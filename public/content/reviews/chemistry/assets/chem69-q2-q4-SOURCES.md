## Original process diagram

`chem69-ea-electron-attachment.svg` is an original process diagram for
Cl(g) + e⁻ → Cl⁻(g). It illustrates electron attachment and the distinction
between a positive amount of energy released and a negative attachment energy
change. It does not depict orbitals or an atomic-size scale.

## Content QA scope

Reviewed only the content in exam sections **2, 4, 5, 6 and 8**, as requested:
definitions, conditions, units, nomenclature, worked-example arithmetic,
recall answers, bilingual equivalence, and the meaning of figure labels.

- 8 main-group families with element symbols and Thai/English names; H and He
  explicitly distinguished. The examples are not presented as all 118 elements.
- EA release convention versus signed attachment change; Cl/F exception;
  IE1 exceptions Be/B and N/O; no blanket monotonic EA arrow.
- Ideal VSEPR angles versus molecule-specific bond angles; electron-region
  geometry versus molecular shape; wedge/dash directions.
- All molecules retain London dispersion; hydrogen-bond donor and acceptor;
  pure-substance forces versus solute–water interactions; property comparisons
  have appropriate size, pressure or temperature conditions.
- Absolute pressure, Kelvin, consistent R units, fixed-variable conditions,
  fixed-n limitation of the combined law; 0.500 mol at 300 K and 1.00 atm
  gives about 12.3 L.
- 10 core functional-group families and 5 selected extensions, alongside
  hydrocarbon parent names; carbonyl connectivity, ester-side order,
  carbon counting for CHO/COOH/CN, N locants, priority, repeated groups,
  aromatic positions, cycloalkene locants and punctuation.
- Both languages contain 54 recall questions / 117 separately spaced subparts;
  exam-section IDs 1–15 and the bank/content identities are preserved.
